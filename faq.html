What is sourdough, and is it different from regular bread?

Sourdough is leavened with a natural fermented starter instead of commercial yeast. The slow rise gives the bread its tangy flavor and chewy, open crumb.

What ingredients do you use?

We use imported Caputo and Poselli flours, King Arthur Select Organic All-Purpose Flour, organic hard red wheat, organic oats, organic vanilla, organic powdered milk, organcic cane, brown, and powdered sugars, organic cocoa, imported Dutch dark and milk chocolate, organic baking powder, organic cornstarch, Redmond Real Salt, farm-fresh eggs, and grass-fed New Zealand butter. The full list is on the menu page.

Do you have allergen information?

Our breads and baked goods are made with wheat, eggs, butter, milk, and some items contain nuts (such as walnuts, cherry nut and pistachio). Everything is made in a kitchen that handles these ingredients, we cannot guarantee no cross contamination. Many organic grains are processed in facilities that may also process soy products.

How should I store my bread?

The best way to store sourdough bread is in a breadbox. If you don't have a breadbox, a paper bag on the counter works well for short term storage. Sourdough freezes well, so cutting and freezing extra slices is always a great option.
