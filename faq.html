What is sourdough, and is it different from regular bread?

Sourdough is leavened with a natural fermented starter instead of commercial yeast. The slow rise gives the bread its tangy flavor and chewy, open crumb.

What ingredients do you use?

We use imported Caputo and Poselli flours, King Arthur Select Organic All-Purpose Flour, organic hard red wheat, oats, vanilla, powdered milk, sugars, cocoa, baking powder and cornstarch, Redmond Real Salt, farm-fresh eggs, and grass-fed New Zealand butter. The full list is on the menu page.

Do you have allergen information?
