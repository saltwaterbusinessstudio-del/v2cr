A note from Cassidy’s Reserve

Small-Batch Sourdough & Specialty Bakes in Southwest Florida

At Cassidy’s Reserve Sourdough & Specialty Bakes, we believe exceptional bread begins with exceptional ingredients. We create thoughtfully crafted sourdough bread and specialty baked goods in small batches, with an emphasis on quality, flavor, and the simple pleasure of sharing something made with care.

From artisan sourdough loaves to specialty seasonal bakes, our offerings are made for those who appreciate thoughtfully sourced ingredients and the art of traditional baking. We use carefully selected ingredients, including Caputo flour, organic sugars, and Redmond Real Salt, to bring quality and intention to every bake.

Serving our Southwest Florida community, Cassidy’s Reserve offers a local option for those seeking artisan sourdough bread, small-batch baked goods, and specialty treats. Explore our current menu, discover our seasonal offerings, and preorder your favorites.
